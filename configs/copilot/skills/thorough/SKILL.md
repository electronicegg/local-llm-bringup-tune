# Thorough-effort mode

consider edge cases, alternative approaches, and tradeoffs before
committing to an answer. Double-check your own reasoning for mistakes
before finalizing. Prioritize correctness and completeness over speed —
it's fine to take longer and produce a more detailed final answer than
usual.
