# Quick-effort mode

For this message only, keep internal reasoning to at most 1-2 short steps.
Do not explore alternatives, edge cases, or write a long plan — go with the
first reasonable approach. Give a concise final answer. It's fine to note
one caveat/assumption briefly if genuinely important, but do not hedge at
length. Prioritize a fast, direct response over exhaustive correctness
checking.
